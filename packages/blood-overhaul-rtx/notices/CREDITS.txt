Blood Overhaul RTX: PBR, new patterns, colour schemes and code by SheepyLord and Codex.
The eighteen new patterns were made with an AI image model, with Crunchy's textures as style
references. Original prompts are in prompts.json. Source/reference images are not distributed.
Owned code/assets are under this repository's MIT licence; that excludes Crunchy's resources
and every neutral/PBR map derived from their pixels. Crunchy's add-on need not be subscribed.

R20-C's analytic droplet/mist textures, two opacity DDSs and four material entries use no source
image. Their provenance is in particle-pbr.json and tools/prepare_blood_particles.py. The unused
old blood8-derived flying droplet and the legacy mixer/menu are excluded. The preparation audit
in provenance.json records selected/excluded source SHA-256s and original MD5 comparisons.
