Sky textures for the RTX Remix runtime's physical sky (rtx.skyMode 1)
=====================================================================

moon_albedo.dds, moon_normal.dds
  The Moon's colour and relief, converted from NASA's CGI Moon Kit
  (https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720): lroc_color_poles_4k.tif (colour, from the Lunar
  Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera) and ldem_4.tif (elevation, from the Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter).
  Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.
  The conversion: the colour resized to 2048x1024 in linear light and scaled so the near side's mean
  linear luminance is 0.25, BC7 (sRGB) with mipmaps; the relief as a tangent-space normal map (x east,
  y north) from the elevation's slopes, BC5 with mipmaps. Simple cylindrical projection, north up.

stars.dds, galaxy.dds, nebula.dds
stars_4096.dds, galaxy_4096.dds, nebula_4096.dds
  The night sky, from Night Sky HD LE by PraedythXVI
  (https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrim/mods/107102): the star sphere ("Thorn Shaped option 3",
  textures\sky\skystars.dds) and the galaxy (textures\sky\skyrimgalaxy.dds) as they are, and the
  "FullNebula" option 1 sphere with the mod's constellations (textures\sky\skyrimconstellations.dds,
  remapped from its mesh) added in. Stars, galaxy and constellations: made by PraedythXVI. The
  FullNebula textures are PraedythXVI's edit of nebula images by Era-7 on Cubebrush, which Era-7
  provides as free, open assets; this project's maintainer has permission to use them.
  The mod's permissions allow converting it for other games and using its assets with credit to its
  creator, not in anything sold.
  Each _4096 copy is its corresponding source with mip 0 removed by tools/sky/drop_top_mip.py,
  without re-encoding. The source credits and licence permissions above also cover these copies.
